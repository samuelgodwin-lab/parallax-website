Accessible & Compliant

Accessible is the law here.

Colorado got there first. HB21-1110 has required every public platform to meet WCAG 2.1 AA since July 2025, at $3,500 a violation; the ADA Title II web rule follows in April 2027, the Privacy Act governs the data and SB 26-189 the AI. Product sold to the public sector has to pass. We measure every page we ship against AA — and can do the same for yours.

Read the Colorado timetable

Audit

Accessibility audits (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Measured page by page against Title II and HB21-1110, every failure specified as a fix rather than a finding.

Fix

Remediation

Contrast, focus, structure, forms and motion brought to AA inside your existing codebase, without a redesign.

Systems

Accessible design systems

Tokens and components that pass by default, so the next feature your team ships is compliant before anyone checks.

Privacy

Privacy by design (CPA)

Consent, opt-outs and data-protection assessments designed into the product the way the Colorado Privacy Act reads them, not bolted on by a banner.

AI

AI disclosure & consent flows (SB 26-189)

Notice, disclosure and impact-assessment flows for the high-risk AI features you’ll ship in 2027, designed in before the law is live.

Handover

Compliance handover

Documentation, VPAT-style conformance reports and test evidence that let a procurement office, or the next vendor, pick it up.
