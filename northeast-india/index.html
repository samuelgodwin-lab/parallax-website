Public Sector

Citizens don't get to choose another portal.

Which is precisely why public software should be held to a higher standard than private, not a lower one. We work with state departments, boards and missions on the systems citizens are required to use, and we hand back a design system the next vendor can build on.

Permits

Tourism & permit systems

Inner-line permits, park entries and registrations, issued online instead of at a barrier in the rain.

Welfare

Scheme & benefit tracking

Enrolment, eligibility and disbursement on one record, so a beneficiary can see where their claim actually is.

Service delivery

Citizen service portals

The counter, the token queue and the triplicate form, rebuilt as something a citizen can finish at home on a phone.

Handover

Design system handover

Components, tokens and documentation the next vendor inherits, so the next procurement cycle doesn't start from nothing.

Operations

Internal dashboards

The screens a department actually runs on, built for the officer reading them at 9am rather than for the annual report.

Compliance

Accessibility audits

WCAG audits of the systems citizens have no choice but to use, with the fixes specified rather than only flagged.

For Designers in the Region

Talent is not the constraint in the Northeast. Access is. We're building the network, the standard and the body of work that keeps designers here — on real products, paid properly, credited publicly. Three ways in.
