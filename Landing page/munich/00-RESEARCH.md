## 1. The market in numbers

- **976,230** people in social-security employment (2025 record, +0.6%). Seven
  DAX-listed companies: automotive, insurance, high-tech, energy.
- **3,622 new startups in 2025** (+24%, above the 2021 boom); **1,358 in Q1 2026**,
  a record quarter. **19.3 startups per 100k residents** — ahead of Berlin (16.8).
- **€2.7bn** venture capital into Munich startups; eight unicorns, two decacorns.
  UnternehmerTUM network alone raised >€1bn in 2025 and is ranked #1 in Europe (FT).
- Sectors with the largest 2025 rounds: security, biotech, fintech, spacetech,
  energy, hospitality.

## 2. What is different from the Northeast

The Northeast page was built on an **access** problem: talent leaves, clients
have never worked with a designer, no local standard. Munich is the inverse.

- **Saturated, expensive supply.** Agencies bill **€100–250/hr**; freelancers
  €45–110, seniors €90–130. Named competition: COBE, SNK (130 staff, B2B CX),
  BB Agency, etvide, &why, N+P Industrial Design.
- **Designers are scarce anyway.** Filling a senior UX role takes **>5 months**;
  **38%** of employers cite missing UX expertise as the reason (up from 17% in
  2023). Average UX salary ~€66k nationally, ~€60–80k in Munich; rents
  €1,200–1,800 for a one-bed. Supply is capped by cost of living.
- **Demand is widening below the agency price point.** Only **30%** of
  Mittelstand firms ran a digitalisation project recently (KfW 2025); the gap
  between pioneers and laggards is widening. 3,600 new startups a year need a
  brand and a product before Series A — at €200/hr they can't have both.
- **The trust bar is higher.** GDPR plus Germany's stricter BDSG; an AVV
  (data-processing agreement) and IP assignment under German law are table
  stakes. India is read as "cheap, for non-sensitive work" — the objection to
  design around is *"will it be as good as a Munich studio, and is it safe?"*
- **Language.** Startups and corporate innovation units run in English. The
  Mittelstand buys in German, owner-to-owner.

So the Munich proposition is not "we bring design where there is none". It is
**studio-quality brand + product + frontend, in one team, at a rate a
Series-A or Mittelstand budget can actually carry — with the paperwork a German
buyer expects.**

## 3. The four sectors for the bento

Chosen for (a) volume of buyers, (b) a concrete product we can name,
(c) reuse of what we already build.

**S–01 · Startups & scale-ups (TUM / UnternehmerTUM)**
Brand system before the seed deck; product UI before the first enterprise
customer; a design system before the second designer. *We build:* brand
systems · product UI · design systems · marketing sites.

**S–02 · Mobility & industrial (incl. Mittelstand)**
Configurators, dealer and fleet portals, B2B ordering, machine HMIs. Owner-led
buying; software that has never been designed, only built. *We build:*
configurators · partner & fleet portals · industrial software UI.

**S–03 · Insurance & finance**
Onboarding, claims, broker and advisor dashboards. Regulated, and since June
2025 banking services must be accessible by law. *We build:* onboarding &
claims flows · broker portals · advisor dashboards.

**S–04 · Real estate & proptech**
Same capability as our Northeast real-estate work, different market: owner,
tenant and management portals; inventory and viewing flows. *We build:*
inventory & viewing portals · tenant apps · property-management UI.

*Considered and parked:* biotech/medtech (regulated, MDR-driven, long sales
cycle — a fifth tile later if a client lands), aerospace (internal tooling,
hard to reach from outside).

## 4. The BFSG band (replaces the Northeast "Government" band)

The **Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz** has applied since **28 June 2025**
(EU Accessibility Act, EN 301 549 → **WCAG 2.1 AA**). It covers B2C websites
and apps, e-commerce, banking services, transport, e-books. Micro-enterprises
(<10 staff, <€2m) are exempt; pure B2B is out of scope.

- Fines **€10,000–100,000**, and — the German twist — **competitor Abmahnungen**
  under unfair-competition law, so enforcement is not only the regulator.
- Accessibility is the **#2 trend** in the German UPA industry report, behind
  generative AI and ahead of design systems.
- Most affected sites are still not compliant. Every insurer, bank, shop and
  mobility operator in the city is in scope.

This is the one thing we already do measurably better than the local market —
every page we ship is contrast-measured to AA. A band that says so, with the
services: **accessibility audits · remediation · accessible design systems ·
handover the next vendor can keep compliant.**

## 5. The network (replaces the "Design hub")

In Munich the story flips. Not "stay here" — **"front it here"**.

- **9.5%** of German UX professionals freelance; their average income is
  €114k on ~124 billed days. Their #1 problem is **client acquisition (63%)**,
  then convincing clients UX matters (47%) and budget (44%).
- We are the opposite shape: a delivery team with capacity, without a
  German-speaking face in the room.

**The Munich partner network:** senior, German-speaking product designers and
design leads who front the client relationship locally — discovery, workshops,
presentations — while the Parallax team delivers. Paid a day rate for their
time and a finder's share for work they bring. No exclusivity. Same three
routes as the Northeast page, re-pointed: *lead a project · bring work in ·
set the standard (portfolio reviews for the network, MCBW / Friends of Figma
presence).*

Community to be present in: **Friends of Figma Munich** (1,380 members),
**Bits & Pretzels 28–30 Sep 2026** (7,500 founders — three weeks from now),
**MCBW 10–16 May 2027**, **IAA Mobility 7–12 Sep 2027**.

## 6. Proof and positioning

- **Munich is where the founder trained.** Samuel studied at Hochschule
  Macromedia (Sandstraße 9, Munich). The BMW portfolio-management project —
  GDPR, data segregation, in-car marketing, personas through prototypes — was
  a Munich brief with BMW as the client. ⚠ It was an interdisciplinary
  university project; how it is described on the page is Samuel's call.
- **Time zone works in our favour.** 09:00–18:00 Munich is 12:30–21:30 in
  Bengaluru — the whole Munich working day is covered live.
- **Trust block, stated plainly:** AVV / DPA as standard · IP assigned under
  German law · EU-hosted tooling on request · English or German-language
  delivery via a Munich lead.
- **Price framing:** never "cheap". "A senior studio at a rate a product
  budget can carry" — and let the €100–250/hr market do the maths.
