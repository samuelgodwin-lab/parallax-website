# Images — Parallax Munich

## v2 brief (15 Sep 2026) — reshot after Dubai

v1 (14 Sep) used the Northeast clause — "cool overcast daylight, restrained
desaturated palette, hard structure" — and every slot came out the same
grey: concrete halls, empty rooms, people from behind or cropped to hands.
The Dubai reshoot set the rule that now applies to every market: the
photography has to be *of the place*, the buyer has to be in the picture,
and each page carries **two colour scenes mixed across both grids** so no
section is one tone.

**What Munich looks like** (research: [simply Munich — architecture A–Z](https://www.munich.travel/en/topics/shopping-design/architectural-highlights-from-a-to-z),
[PicturaVera — architecture photo spots](https://www.picturavera.de/fotoblog/10-muenchen-architektur-fotospots),
[Studio Fou — Werksviertel](https://www.studiofou.com/architecture/werksviertel-munich),
[Munich Tourism — architecture](https://www.munichtourism.org/munich-architecture/)):

- **Light:** clear and crisp, not overcast — Föhn-blue sky, sharp shadows,
  the Alps visible on a good day. Blue hour is long and deep, and the
  Altbau windows glow warm against it.
- **Palette:** cream, ochre and pale-green stucco (Maxvorstadt, Schwabing),
  pale limestone (Königsplatz), chestnut green, oak and walnut inside,
  brass lamps; the new quarters — Werksviertel, Fünf Höfe — are red brick,
  black steel and glass done carefully, "structural clarity, rhythmic
  repetition, a strong interplay of light and shadow."
- **People:** precise and prosperous — the insurer in a navy suit, the
  Mittelstand engineer in a grey work shirt, the TUM founder in a T-shirt,
  the couple buying the Altbau flat. Faces, not backs.
- **Places:** a Werksviertel brick hall, an automotive development hall,
  an insurance HQ boardroom, a Maxvorstadt street, Altbau offices with tall
  windows and parquet, a studio under a sawtooth roof.
- **Keep from v1:** the cool register is honest for Munich — it just can't
  be *only* grey. The hero video and the network image (designers with the
  Frauenkirche through the glass) are untouched.

**Bright clause (Munich):** "editorial architectural photography for
Munich, crisp clear Alpine daylight under a bright blue sky, cream and ochre
stucco, pale limestone, warm oak with glass and steel, chestnut green,
precise, calm and prosperous, no logos, no text."

**Dark clause (Munich):** "editorial photography for Munich at blue hour,
cool deep-blue dusk through tall windows, warm lamp and screen light on
oak, brass and steel, charcoal and navy palette, precise, calm and
prosperous, no logos, no text."

Mix: sectors **B D / D B**, band **B D B / D B D** — the same grid pattern
as Dubai. Generated with `nano_banana_pro` at 2k, centre-cropped to slot,
WebP q80 + JPEG q82. All ten accepted first pass. v1 files are overwritten
in place (copies in the session scratchpad).

## v1 record (14 Sep 2026)

Generated with `nano_banana_pro` at 2k on the shared Northeast clause, then
centre-cropped and resampled. Hero: a high-key overcast exterior, replaced
the same day by the Vimeo hero; `muc-hero-poster` is its first frame.
Sectors: startup floor from behind · technician at an HMI · corporate
atrium · Altbau street (first take was a raw concrete block that read as
Eastern-bloc). Band: trackpad hand · developer from behind · component
wall · report across a table (hands only) · a pile of statements · an
empty dashboard room. Network: v1 was a client presentation with a
wireframe wall, replaced by the designers-in-motion take that stays.
