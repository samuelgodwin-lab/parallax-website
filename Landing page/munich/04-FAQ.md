**Is Parallax a design agency in Munich?**
Parallax is a brand, product and frontend design studio serving Munich, founded by a designer who trained in Munich. Each project gets a dedicated team built out from our Munich hub, with the lead on the ground for discovery, workshops and presentations. The studio builds brand systems, product interfaces and marketing sites for Munich's startups, mobility and industrial companies, insurers and property developers.

**Do you work in German?**
Discovery and presentations are run in English today, and in German as Munich-based leads join the studio's network. German UI copy, documentation and the Barrierefreiheitserklärung are produced with native German speakers; design files and code are delivered in English.

**Is your work BFSG-compliant?**
Yes. Since 28 June 2025 the Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz requires consumer websites, apps, shops and banking services to meet WCAG 2.1 AA, with fines up to €100,000 and competitor Abmahnungen. Parallax measures every page it ships against AA and audits, remediates and documents existing products to the same standard.

**What does a brand identity or product design cost from Parallax?**
Parallax publishes its prices. Foundation starts at €12,000 for a brand identity plus one digital touchpoint; Studio starts at €28,000 for a complete brand system, product UI and a frontend build. Munich agencies typically quote well above that scope-for-scope; the difference is a Bengaluru delivery team behind a Munich lead.

**Which industries in Munich do you design for?**
Four sectors sit at the centre of the Munich practice: startups and scale-ups that need a brand system and product UI before the next round; mobility and industrial companies with configurators and engineering tools; insurance and finance onboarding that customers finish; and real-estate and proptech portals for owners, tenants and managers.

**How does a studio in Bengaluru work with a Munich team?**
The dedicated project team is built out from the Munich hub and works in your hours, with the lead on the ground for reviews and workshops; the studio continues after you log off, so the next iteration is ready the following morning. 09:00 in Munich is 12:30 in Bengaluru, so the two working days overlap until the German evening.
