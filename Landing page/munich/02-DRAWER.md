# Network drawer — Munich variant

Same drawer as Northeast; the seven perks, the three steps and the chips are
re-pointed at a German-speaking lead rather than a designer joining a team.
Title: **The studio behind you. / The room in front.**
Perks: A day rate, paid on time · Your name on the work · A full team behind
you · Real products in your portfolio · 10% of what you bring in · First look
at roles · Studio tools while you're on (with the 09:00 Munich = 12:30
Bengaluru line). Chips for: Brand · Product · Design leadership ·
German-speaking · 5+ years, or a portfolio that argues otherwise · In or near
Munich. Form gains a hidden `market=Munich` and a "Design leadership" option;
the API routes the subject accordingly.

The page is generated from the Northeast working copy by `build.py` — re-run
it after any structural change to the Northeast page, then re-apply Munich
edits made directly to `munich/index.html` (there are none yet).
