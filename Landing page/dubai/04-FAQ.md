**Is Parallax a design agency in Dubai?**
Parallax is a brand, product and frontend design studio working in Dubai, with two of its own people already in Dubai. Each project gets a dedicated team built out from our Dubai hub, with the lead on the ground to run discovery, presentations and the relationship. The studio builds bilingual Arabic–English brand systems, product interfaces and marketing sites for developers, hospitality groups, fintechs and founders.

**Do you design in Arabic and English?**
Yes. Parallax designs bilingual from the first screen: one component library in two directions, layouts mirrored rather than flipped, Arabic typography set to sit with the Latin, and right-to-left tested on real devices. Arabic localisation is priced on top of the published tiers.

**What does branding or product design cost in Dubai?**
Parallax publishes its Dubai prices in dirhams. Foundation starts at AED 50,000 for a brand identity plus one digital touchpoint; Studio starts at AED 120,000 for a complete brand system, product UI and a frontend build. Both are "from" prices; bilingual scope is quoted separately.

**Are your products PDPL-compliant and accessible?**
Parallax designs consent, records of processing and breach paths into the product under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, and measures every page against WCAG 2.1 AA, the TDRA policy and the Dubai Universal Design Code. UAE Pass and Dubai Now integration are wired on the front end the customer sees.

**Which industries in Dubai do you design for?**
Four sectors sit at the centre of the Dubai practice: real estate and proptech, from off-plan launch sites to owner portals; hospitality and tourism, with direct booking engines and guest apps; fintech onboarding and KYC flows people finish; and founders and trade — brands, storefronts and B2B ordering portals for new licences.

**How do I start a project with Parallax in Dubai?**
Email projects@parallaxorg.com or open a WhatsApp thread with what you're building. The studio starts by questioning the brief on one call, no deck, and someone from the studio can be in a room in Dubai within the week. The dedicated project team is built out from the Dubai hub and works in your hours; 09:00 in Dubai is 10:30 in Bengaluru, so the whole working day overlaps.
