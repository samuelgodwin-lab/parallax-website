## 1. The market in numbers

- UAE GDP **$517bn in 2025 (+6.2%)**, non-oil **+6.8%** to $408bn; non-oil is
  **79.4%** of the economy. Dubai GDP AED 232bn in Q1 2026; Emirates NBD
  forecasts **4.5%** growth for 2026.
- **D33**: double the economy by 2033; 100 projects incl. a unified licence,
  Sandbox Dubai, a 30-unicorn scale-up programme.
- Business licences 2023–25 up **84%** on 2020–22. **Online retail led new
  registrations at ~30%.**
- Startups: **3,500–3,800** active; **$13bn** cumulative investment; UAE took
  **66.5%** of all MENA venture capital in Q1 2026 ($626m across 46 deals).
  DIFC FinTech Hive, in5 (TECOM: Internet City, Media City, d3), Dubai Future
  Accelerators.
- Fastest-growing sector Q1 2026: **finance & insurance, +17.3%**.

## 2. What is different from Munich and the Northeast

**Being Indian is the network here, not the objection.**
- **4.36 million Indians** in the UAE, more than half in Dubai.
- **264,000+ Indian businesses** in the UAE; **22,000** new ones in H1 2025;
  **one in five** UAE businesses is Indian-led; Indian firms are the #1
  nationality joining Dubai Chamber (9,000+ in H1 2025, +14.9%); ~4,000 in
  DMCC alone.
- India–UAE **CEPA** (2022): non-oil trade past **$50bn**.
- **58 flights a week** Bengaluru–Dubai; **+1.5h** time zone — the whole
  Dubai day is live in Bengaluru, and a lead can be in the room by lunch.

In Munich the objection was "is an Indian studio safe and as good?". In Dubai
the buyer is often Indian, has worked with Indian teams, and is deciding on
speed, taste and price. The pitch is **"the studio the Bengaluru founders in
Dubai already know, at Dubai pace."**

**The agency market is bimodal.** Network shops at the top (AKQA, Landor,
Serviceplan Middle East, Tonic), and a long tail of "web design company Dubai"
outfits publishing AED 13k branding packages. Mid-range branding packages run
**AED 50–150k**; a quality identity **AED 15–40k**; agency UX at **AED 100–250/hr**.
Pricing is *published* here — Dubai agencies list packages in AED. The gap is
the same as Munich's: studio quality at a founder's budget, with one team.

**Every product in Dubai is two products.** Arabic and English, RTL and LTR.
For government it is law; for consumer brands it is expected; agencies mostly
bolt Arabic on afterwards. Built-in bilingual design is a differentiator.

**Decisions are fast and personal.** Owner-led companies, WhatsApp as the
channel, references over process. Trust signals: who you've worked with,
who introduced you, whether you'll be in the room.

## 4. The band: bilingual and accessible by default (replaces Government / BFSG)

- Federal and Dubai digital services must be **Arabic and English**, full
  **RTL**, with proper language attributes and Arabic screen-reader support.
- Accessibility: Federal Law 29/2006, the **National Digital Accessibility
  Policy**, TDRA's **WCAG 2.1 AA** web policy, the **Dubai Universal Design
  Code**, and the **UAE Design System 2.0** (designsystem.gov.ae) for federal
  entities.
- **PDPL** (Federal Decree-Law 45/2021, in force 2022): consent, records of
  processing, breach notification; executive regulations still pending, so
  enforcement is ramping through 2025–26 — buyers are nervous and want a
  supplier who has read it.

The band: **"Two languages. One system."** — bilingual design systems (RTL
mirrored, not flipped), Arabic typography done properly, WCAG 2.1 AA measured,
PDPL-aware data flows, UAE Pass integration. Six tiles: Bilingual design
systems · Arabic typography & RTL · Accessibility audits (WCAG 2.1 AA) ·
UAE Pass & Dubai Now integration · PDPL-ready data flows · Handover to the
next vendor.

## 5. The network (Dubai partner network)

Same shape as Munich — **front it here** — with two roles instead of one:
- **Leads** who run the room: discovery, pitch, relationship. Day rate + 10%.
- **Arabic-speaking designers** for localisation and Arabic typography, paid
  per project.

Dubai has a deep expat design bench (agency alumni, freelancers on their own
visas) and an active **Friends of Figma Dubai** (breakfast meetups, Config
watch party, "Do It With Make"). Events: **Dubai Design Week 3–8 Nov 2026**
(d3), **GITEX Global 7–11 Dec 2026** (Expo City), Expand North Star.

## 6. Proof and positioning

- The main site already says "clients in … Dubai". ⚠ Which project(s) —
  and can they be shown? A Dubai logo in the strip matters more here than
  anywhere: references are the currency.
- Time zone: 09:00 Dubai = 10:30 Bengaluru. Fully live.
- Trust block: PDPL-aware by default · bilingual by default · a lead in Dubai
  within a day · WhatsApp-fast.
- Price framing: Dubai publishes prices. Consider showing the main site's
  tiers converted to AED, or a "from AED" line — the market expects it.
- Avoid: skyline clichés (Burj, desert, gold), "luxury" as a value, stock
  handshakes. Dubai in these images is offices, sites, hotels, ports — an
  economy, not a postcard. Same rule as the Northeast brief.
