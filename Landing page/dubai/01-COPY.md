## 10 — Pricing (NEW for Dubai — Dubai publishes prices)
**Slabel:** `PRICING`
**H2:** Transparent pricing. No *surprises.*
**Lede:** Dubai publishes its prices. So do we — three ways to work with us,
in dirhams, with what's included on one page.
Three cards on the main site's `.pricing` pattern (dark band, drawer on
click). Card copy as the main site; only the figures change.

**Card 1 · Starter · Foundation** — Brand identity + single digital
touchpoint. Ideal for early-stage companies that need to move fast without
sacrificing quality.
**Card 2 · Most popular · Studio** — Complete brand system, product UI, and
build-ready design specifications. Our most requested engagement.
**Card 3 · Enterprise · Custom** — `Let's talk`. Multi-phase engagements,
embedded design teams, and long-term partnerships. Scoped to your exact needs.

**The figures — two options, pick one:**

| | Foundation | Studio | How it's set |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Converted (recommended)** | **AED 50,000** | **AED 120,000** | €12,000 / €28,000 at ≈4.30 AED per euro (51,600 / 120,400), rounded down to the nearest 10k so it reads as a price, not a conversion. Same offer, same money, in every market — a buyer with a Munich office sees one studio, not two price lists. |
| **B · Market-set** | **AED 45,000** | **AED 110,000** | From the Dubai check in 00-RESEARCH.md: a quality identity alone runs AED 15–40k, so identity + one touchpoint sits just above it; mid-range branding packages run AED 50–150k *without* product UI, so a brand system + product UI + specs lands at the middle of that range and still undercuts it on scope. |

> Recommend **A**. It's within 10% of B anyway, and it keeps the studio on one
> price list. Check the rate on the day of build; if the euro has moved more
> than 5%, re-round. Either option prefixes the figure with "from" in the
> card (`from AED 50,000 / project`) — Dubai buyers expect a starting price,
> and it leaves room for the bilingual scope, which is extra work.

**Button:** `Start a project` → #cta
