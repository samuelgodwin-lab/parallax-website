# Images v2 — Parallax Northeast India

Reshot 15 Sep 2026, after Dubai and Munich. Supersedes the image half of
`03-IMAGE-BRIEF.md`, which stays as the v1 record. Hero video and poster
and the designers image are untouched.

## Why

v1 ran the studio's "architectural over lifestyle, cool overcast,
desaturated" clause: a construction frame, a cleanroom, a lecture hall
from the back, and a government band of queues, counters and a check post.
Accurate, and bleak — it showed the region's problems, not its people.
The rule now: photography *of the place*, the person the product serves in
the picture, and **two colour scenes mixed across both grids**. Samuel's
call for the band: **citizens being served**, not the teams building the
systems.

## What the region looks like

(Research: [Getty — North East India hills](https://www.gettyimages.in/photos/north-east-india-hills),
[Guwahati monsoon photo spots](https://travel.india.com/guide/destination/top-5-photography-spots-in-guwahati-city-that-look-stunning-during-monsoon-7842628/),
[StayVista — the Northeast in the monsoon](https://www.stayvista.com/blog/places-to-visit-northeast-india-during-the-monsoon/),
[Nexplore — Northeast photography tours](https://nexplore.org/photography-tour.php).)

- **Light:** soft and bright after rain, not flat grey — mist drifting
  through layered green hills, the Brahmaputra "reflecting the clouds",
  Gangtok's hillside houses under monsoon sky. Dusk is a deep blue-grey
  with the scattered lights of a hill town and warm bulbs inside.
- **Palette:** every green there is — pine, tea, bamboo, paddy — against
  whitewash and pastel concrete, timber and tin, and one splash of woven
  colour: the red of a gamosa or a mekhela chador, a Khasi jainsem.
- **People:** Assamese, Khasi, Sikkimese; women prominent (the field
  officer, the counter officer, the senior official — Meghalaya is
  matrilineal and its markets are run by women). Faces, warmth, dignity.
- **Places:** a Guwahati balcony over the river, a Shillong hill lodge, a
  Sikkim pharma control room, a whitewashed campus in the pines, a permit
  counter in Gangtok, a village hall in Assam, a citizen service centre by
  the tea gardens.

**Bright clause (Northeast):** "editorial photography for Northeast India,
soft bright post-monsoon light, mist over green hills, lush green, tea and
bamboo, whitewashed and pastel concrete, timber and tin, a single splash of
woven colour, dignified and hopeful, no logos, no text."

**Dark clause (Northeast):** "editorial photography for Northeast India at
monsoon dusk, deep blue-grey sky and mist over the hills, warm lamp and
screen light on timber and whitewash, hillside lights in the distance,
dignified and hopeful, no logos, no text."

Mix: sectors **B D / D B**, band **B D B / D B D**. Generated with
`nano_banana_pro` at 2k, centre-cropped to slot, WebP q80 + JPEG q82. All
ten accepted first pass; v1 files overwritten in place (copies in the
session scratchpad).
