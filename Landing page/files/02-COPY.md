## 04 — The gap

**Slabel:** `THE GAP`

**H2:**
The region has plenty of people who design the poster. Almost nobody designs the *product.*

**Body:**
Search for a design agency in Guwahati or Shillong and you get logos, social creatives,
video edits and SEO retainers. That work has a market and it is well served.

Interface design and design systems — the layer that decides whether software holds up
past a thousand users — is close to unserved across all eight states. Meanwhile the region's
largest employers are running enterprise operations on screens nobody ever designed.

We don't redesign. We rearchitect.

## 06 — Sectors

### S—01 · Real estate

**H3:** Crores of inventory, sold over *WhatsApp.*

**Body:** Developers across Guwahati and the hill capitals are moving high-value inventory
through PDF brochures and message threads. The buyer moved online years ago. The interface
never followed.

**Tags:** `Inventory dashboards` `Channel partner portals` `Booking & payment flows`

**Datum:** **Brochure → portal** / The transition most developers here haven't made

---

### S—02 · Hospitality

**H3:** Every aggregator booking costs you *twenty percent.*

**Body:** Hotel groups, resorts and homestay networks hand a fifth of each booking to travel
platforms because their own booking flow is unusable. A direct engine that works pays for
itself inside a season.

**Tags:** `Direct booking` `Property management UI` `Guest apps`

**Datum:** **90.55 lakh** / Domestic tourist visits to Assam in 2025 — the region's largest inflow

---

### S—03 · Pharma

**H3:** India's cleanest manufacturing belt, running on its worst *interfaces.*

**Body:** Sikkim holds one of the densest pharmaceutical manufacturing clusters in the
country. Batch records, distributor systems and field-force apps built to satisfy an audit,
never to be used.

**Tags:** `Compliance dashboards` `Distributor portals` `Field-force apps`

**Datum:** **50+ plants** / Sun Pharma, Cipla, Alkem, Glenmark, Zydus, Intas and Macleods among them

---

### S—04 · Education

**H3:** Ten thousand students, one admissions portal, zero *design.*

**Body:** Universities and institutes running admissions, records and learning systems that
students fight rather than use. The highest-volume, highest-friction software in the region,
and the least considered.

**Tags:** `Admissions portals` `Student dashboards` `Learning platforms`

**Datum:** **IIT · NIT · NEHU** / Guwahati, Silchar, Shillong, Tezpur and Nagaland — a dense institutional belt

## Open items — resolve before publishing

1. **Pricing.** The main site lists €12,000 and €28,000 tiers. That is roughly ₹12 lakh and
   ₹30 lakh against a regional market anchored at ₹2,000 logos. This page should either not
   link to the pricing section, or the site needs a rupee entry tier. Nothing in the copy
   solves this.
2. **Client list.** The guidelines name BMW, Biblica, Sportradar, Vestas, Nissan and
   Accenture. The live site shows a partly different set. Confirm which list is cleared
   for public use before the statistics band goes live.
3. **Local presence.** The footer names eight states but the studio is openly Bengaluru.
   Against Brand Imprint's "Northeast Native" positioning, that's the weak flank. A Guwahati
   address or a named regional partner would close it.
