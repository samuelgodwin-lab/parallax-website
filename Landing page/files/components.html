09 .reveal / .stagger

12 Footer

Accent ground, matching live parallaxorg.com. The only place the accent is used as a large fill — the brand guidelines forbid it, the live build does it, and matching the site was the explicit call. White-on-accent measures 5.16:1 here versus 3.90:1 on the live site, because --accent was darkened during the contrast pass. The full stop is not tinted on this ground, since accent-on-accent would erase it.
