## 1. The market in numbers

- Metro Denver GDP **≈ $215bn**; the metro is ~3.3m people, the state 5.9m.
  Companies keep relocating in (Usaj's 2026 relocation report runs to dozens
  of HQ moves a year).
- **Startups:** ~**1,480** active in Denver, ecosystem growth **+13.8% in
  2026**, StartupBlink **#31 globally**; Denver–Boulder is Startup Genome's
  **#15 global ecosystem**, ahead of DC and Miami. Total startup funding
  **$2.32bn**; Colorado VC assets under management **> $5bn** (2025);
  late-stage (Series C+) **> $1.2bn** in 2025. Denver ranks **5th nationally**
  for funding rates and is a top-three US city for "second-wave" tech
  startups — companies that leave the Bay Area for cost and talent.
- **Leading sectors 2026:** aerospace, renewable energy, fintech. Colorado
  aerospace took a record **$23bn** in federal contracts; aerospace job
  postings **+47%** in a year, median pay ≈ $125k.
- **Outdoor recreation:** **$37bn** in consumer spending and **511,000**
  direct jobs statewide; The North Face, Vail Resorts, Osprey, Boa are here,
  and the state is fighting to keep the Outdoor Retailer HQ in Denver.
- **Health tech:** **425+** health-tech companies in Denver (SonderMind,
  CirrusMD, Strive Health); the Anschutz medical campus in Aurora.
- **Fintech:** **187** fintech companies HQ'd in Denver, **$1.5bn** raised
  across 59 funded (Alviere, Infinicept; Gusto's second HQ).
- **Real estate 2026:** median home price **$575k** (down from $604k as the
  mix shifts to townhomes and condos); downtown condo zips at **120 days of
  supply** — a buyer's market with HOA fees the sticking point. Sellers and
  developers need product that moves inventory, not brochures.

## 2. What is different from Dubai, Munich and the Northeast

**The time zone is the whole problem — and the whole pitch.** Denver is
UTC−6 (MDT) / −7 (MST): **09:00 Denver = 20:30 Bengaluru**. There is no
working-day overlap. Dubai was "the whole day is live"; Munich was "your
afternoon is our evening"; Denver is **follow-the-sun**: the client briefs
at the end of their day, the studio builds overnight, the work is waiting
at 8am Mountain. Sold honestly, that is a feature — a 24-hour cycle — but
it *requires* the lead in the room in Denver more than in any other market.
The network section is not a nice-to-have here; it is the delivery model.

**No flight is short.** Bengaluru–Denver is 20h+ with one stop (Lufthansa
via Frankfurt, United via Newark/SFO, Emirates via Dubai); no direct. A
founder visit is a planned week, not a lunch.

**The Indian network is thinner than Dubai's, but it is the tech one.**
~39,000 Indians in Colorado (0.7%), ~4,100 in Denver proper, concentrated
in the DTC / Lone Tree / Aurora corridor and in tech; the Network of Indian
Professionals has a chapter. Being an Indian studio is neither the
objection (Munich) nor the network (Dubai); it is *normal* — every Denver
tech company already works with an Indian team somewhere in its stack.
The pitch is not "we are Indian", it is "we are the design half your
offshore stack never had".

**Denver buys taste.** Outdoor and consumer brands set the visual bar; RiNo
is a creative district of converted warehouses, galleries and agencies;
AIGA Colorado is active; Colorado Startup Week (14–18 Sep 2026, 10,000
people, free) is the largest of its kind in North America. Portfolio and
craft matter more here than in Munich (process) or Dubai (references).

**Agency pricing is high and published.** Denver hourly rates run
**$90–175**, branding agencies **$100–149/h**; a logo project **$5–50k**, a
small-business site **$10–35k+**, a 5-page agency site **$9–22k**, a full
identity **$5–20k** from a boutique, **$100k+** for identity plus e-commerce.
The gap is the same as everywhere: studio quality at a founder's price,
one team from identity to shipped code.

**It is a second-wave city, not a first-wave one.** Companies here left
San Francisco and New York for cost. They are receptive to a studio that
is *also* the better-value option — as long as the work looks like it came
from the coast.

## 4. The band: accessible and compliant by default (replaces BFSG / bilingual)

Three laws, one message: **US buyers have been sued into caring.**

- **ADA Title II web rule** (DOJ, April 2024): every state and local
  government website, app and course material must meet **WCAG 2.1 AA**.
  Deadline extended 20 Apr 2026 to **26 Apr 2027** (entities serving 50k+)
  and **26 Apr 2028** (under 50k). Litigation risk did not move with it.
- **Colorado HB21-1110**: the state's own accessibility law — all state
  and local government digital platforms to WCAG 2.1 AA, deadline
  **1 Jul 2025** (already passed); non-compliance carries a private right
  of action and statutory damages of $3,500 per violation to the affected
  person. Colorado is ahead of the federal rule, not behind it.
- **Colorado Privacy Act** (in force July 2023): consent, opt-outs, data
  protection assessments — the state's PDPL equivalent.
- **Colorado AI Act** (SB 24-205): the first US state AI law, delayed to
  30 Jun 2026, paused by a federal court in April, and **replaced on 14 May
  2026 by SB 26-189**, effective **1 Jan 2027** — a lighter, US-style regime
  for "high-risk" AI in employment, housing, lending and health decisions.
  Product teams shipping AI features in 2027 will need disclosure and
  impact-assessment flows designed in.

The band: **"Accessible is the law here."** Six tiles: Accessibility
audits (WCAG 2.1 AA) · Remediation · Accessible design systems · Privacy
by design (CPA) · AI disclosure & consent flows (SB 26-189) · Compliance
handover. ⚠ Alternative heading: *"Built for Title II. Ready for 2027."*

## 6. Proof and positioning

- The main site says "clients in Munich, Dubai and Denver". ⚠ **Which Denver
  client?** Nothing under `Projects/` is obviously US-based (Alice Media,
  Axel, BMW, NomNomGo, Suka, YVT). If there is none, the strip leads with
  BMW and the hero proof line drops the "already in Denver" claim — do not
  invent one.
- Trust block: a lead in Denver, in your time zone · work waiting at 8am
  Mountain · WCAG 2.1 AA measured · one studio, identity to shipped code.
- Price framing: Denver publishes prices in USD. Show the tiers in USD on
  the page as Dubai does in AED. Conversion at ~1.17 USD/EUR: €12,000 →
  **$14,000**, €28,000 → **$33,000** (rounded). Market check says a
  boutique identity alone is $5–20k and a 5-page site $9–22k, so Foundation
  at $14k (identity + one touchpoint) and Studio at $33k (brand system +
  product UI + build) sit *below* local agency scope-for-scope — which is
  the point. ⚠ Confirm USD tiers.
- Images (per the rule set 15 Sep): of the place, the buyer in the picture,
  two scenes mixed across both grids. Denver's register: **high-altitude
  light** — hard, clear, blue sky, long shadows; the Front Range as the
  horizon; RiNo brick and murals, LoDo warehouses, Union Station; timber,
  steel, concrete; fleece and flannel, not suits. Dark scene: alpenglow /
  blue hour with the mountains and the city lights. Avoid: ski postcards,
  cannabis, cowboy kitsch, the Red Rocks cliché.
