# Images — Parallax Denver

## The brief (17 Sep 2026)

The rule set on Dubai, applied from the start: photography *of the place*,
the buyer in the picture, and two colour scenes mixed across both grids —
sectors B D / D B, band B D B / D B D — so no grid is one tone and no
section stands apart.

Denver's register, from `00-RESEARCH.md` §6: **high-altitude light** —
hard, clear, deep blue sky, long shadows; the Front Range as the horizon;
RiNo brick and murals, LoDo warehouses, Union Station; timber, blackened
steel, concrete; fleece and flannel, not suits. Dark scene: **alpenglow /
blue hour** — the mountains lit pink through the glass, city lights and
screens glowing. Avoid: ski postcards, cannabis, cowboy kitsch, the Red
Rocks cliché.

**Style clause (Denver, bright):** "editorial architectural photography for
Denver, Colorado, hard clear high-altitude daylight, deep blue sky, long
crisp shadows, warm red brick, timber, blackened steel and concrete palette,
clean spacious interiors, no logos, no text."

**Style clause (Denver, dark):** "editorial photography for Denver, Colorado
at blue hour, alpenglow on the Front Range through the glass, city lights
and screens glowing, charcoal, navy and warm brick palette with amber lamp
highlights, clean spacious interiors, no logos, no text."

Generated with `nano_banana_pro` at 2k (served as nano_banana_2),
centre-cropped to slot, WebP q80 + JPEG q82. All eleven accepted first
pass. Originals in the session scratchpad (`den/`).
