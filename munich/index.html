Accessibility

Accessible is no longer a choice.

Since 28 June 2025 the Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz requires every consumer website, app, shop and banking service to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Fines run to €100,000 — and a competitor can send the Abm­ahnung before the regulator ever looks. We measure every page we ship against AA. We can do the same for the ones you already have.

Audit

Accessibility audits

WCAG 2.1 AA measured page by page, with every failure specified as a fix rather than a finding.

Fix

Remediation

Contrast, focus, structure, forms and motion brought to AA inside your existing codebase, without a redesign.

System

Accessible design systems

Tokens and components that pass by default, so the next feature your team ships is compliant before anyone checks.

Handover

Compliance handover

The documentation, the Barrierefreiheitserklärung and the test evidence a Marktüberwachung or an Abmahnung will ask for.

Documents

Accessible documents & PDFs

Tagged, navigable, readable by a screen reader — the contracts, statements and brochures the law counts as part of the service.

Ongoing

Ongoing monitoring

Quarterly re-checks as content and code change, so you stay compliant rather than having been compliant once.
